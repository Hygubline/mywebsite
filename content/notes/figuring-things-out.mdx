I used to think a personal website had to be a résumé with nicer fonts. A place to prove things. Lately I've stopped believing that.

This is the first note in what I'm thinking of as a *garden* rather than a portfolio. The difference matters to me.

## A portfolio is finished. A garden is alive.

A portfolio says *here is what I have done.* It's tidy, frozen, optimized for someone else's judgment. A garden says *here is what I'm tending right now* — some things half-grown, some experiments that didn't work, a few ideas I keep coming back to.

I want the second thing. A place where I can:

- write down half-formed thoughts before they evaporate
- collect interface ideas that make me feel something
- build tiny animations just to see if I can
- keep notes on what I read so I actually remember it

## Notes, not essays

I'm deliberately calling these *notes*. An essay feels like it needs a thesis and a conclusion. A note can just be a thing I noticed on a Tuesday.

Lowering the bar is the whole point. The work that compounds is the work you actually keep doing.

> You don't have to finish the thought to write it down. You just have to start.

So this is the start. More to come, slowly.
