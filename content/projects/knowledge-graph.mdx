title: Personal Knowledge Graph
subtitle: "A note system where ideas are nodes, and the links are the point"
overview: "A note-taking tool that stores ideas as a graph instead of folders — notes become nodes, connections become edges, and a D3 visualization lets me explore my own thinking as a map."
why: "My notes kept dying in folders. I never reread them because nothing connected to anything. I wanted to *see* my own thinking as a web, and stumble back into old ideas by accident."
role: "Solo build across the stack — a React + D3 front end for the graph view, and a Python/FastAPI backend over Neo4j to store and query the connections."
learned: "The value was never in storing information — it was in surfacing relationships I'd forgotten existed. Building the graph changed what I was able to notice about my own notes."
nextStep: "The auto-linking was too eager and drew noisy connections. Next, I'd have it *suggest* links and let me confirm, rather than deciding for me."
