Goals are seductive. They give us clarity. A target. Something to aim for.

But goals are also dangerous. They create a binary: success or failure. They focus on outcomes we can't fully control.

## The System Alternative

A system is a repeatable process that moves you in the right direction regardless of any single outcome.

- **Goal**: Publish 12 essays this year
- **System**: Write for 30 minutes every morning

The goal depends on external validation. The system depends only on you showing up.

## Why Systems Win

Systems win because they're:

**Resilient** — A missed goal feels like failure. A missed day in a system is just a data point.

**Compounding** — Each iteration improves the system itself. Goals don't improve; they just get replaced.

**Sustainable** — Goals end. Systems continue. Which one builds a career?

## The Identity Shift

Goals are about having. Systems are about being.

"I want to be a writer" vs "I want to have written a book."

The first is an identity. The second is an achievement. Achievements fade. Identities compound.

## Designing Your System

A good system has:

- **Clear triggers** — When does it activate?
- **Minimal friction** — How do you remove obstacles?
- **Built-in feedback** — How do you know it's working?

Don't ask "what do I want to achieve?" Ask "who do I want to become, and what system would that person run?"

Then run that system. Every day. Forever.
