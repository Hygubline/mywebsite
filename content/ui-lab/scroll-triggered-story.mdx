I love the scrollytelling sections you see in good editorial sites — where a graphic stays pinned while captions step through it, beat by beat. I want to build one with GSAP's `ScrollTrigger` and `pin`.

## Open questions

- How much to pin without trapping the reader.
- Designing the fallback for short viewports and reduced-motion.
- Finding a small *story* worth telling this way — the technique is easy; the content is the hard part.

A someday project I'm excited about.
