Three overlapping radial gradients on an oversized background, animated by shifting `background-position`. No JavaScript — just CSS keyframes panning the mesh from one corner to another and back.

## What I learned

- Keep it *slow*. An 18-second loop reads as ambient; a 4-second loop reads as a screensaver.
- Layering a faint solid overlay on top keeps text readable no matter where the gradient drifts.
- Warm + a single cool accent (here, a whisper of iris) feels richer than a rainbow.

This is the same mesh that glows behind the hero and the preview tiles in this lab.
