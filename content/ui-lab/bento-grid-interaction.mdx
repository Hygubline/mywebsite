The portal on this site's home page. Each tile is a glass card that leans subtly toward the cursor (a gentle magnetic pull) while a radial glow tracks the exact point you're hovering.

## Where it stands

The core is working — magnetic motion plus a pointer-driven glow. I'm still figuring out:

- how to make the staggered scroll-in feel less mechanical
- whether the larger "feature" tile should animate differently from the small ones
- keeping the whole grid calm when the cursor moves fast across many tiles at once

A small study in making a navigation grid feel like a *place* rather than a menu.
