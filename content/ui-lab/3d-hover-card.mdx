Map the cursor's position over the card to a pair of rotation values, apply them with `perspective` and `transformStyle: preserve-3d`, and add a radial glare that follows the same coordinates. A spring smooths the motion so it never feels twitchy.

## What I learned

- `perspective` lives on the *parent*, not the card itself — easy to forget.
- The glare sells the depth more than the rotation does. Without it the tilt looks like a flat skew.
- Clamp the rotation to ~15–18°. Past that it stops reading as "a card" and starts reading as "a glitch".
