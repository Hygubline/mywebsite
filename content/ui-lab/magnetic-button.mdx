A button doesn't need to sit still. This one tracks the cursor while you hover nearby and gently translates toward it, with the label drifting a little further than the container so it feels like it has weight.

## The idea

The whole effect is two numbers: the cursor's offset from the button's center, scaled down. Move the element a fraction of that offset, move the label a slightly larger fraction, and let a spring handle the return.

## What I learned

- The *spring* on the way back matters more than the follow on the way in. A linear reset feels cheap; an overshoot feels alive.
- Clamping the pull distance keeps it from sliding off into the void on a big screen.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — the effect should simply not run for people who opt out.
