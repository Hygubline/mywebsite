The reveal that opens almost every page here. Each word sits inside a thin overflow-hidden wrapper and starts pushed 120% below the baseline; GSAP slides them up with a soft `power4.out` ease and a small stagger.

## Why I like it

Text that simply fades in feels flat. Text that *rises into place* feels like it's arriving — like someone is setting the words down in front of you.

## What I learned

- Wrap each word in its own clipping mask so the upward motion is hidden until the word lands.
- Keep the stagger small (40–60ms). Anything slower starts to feel like a loading screen.
- `yPercent` is friendlier than pixel offsets — it scales with the font size automatically.
