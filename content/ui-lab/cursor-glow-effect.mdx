A fixed, full-screen radial gradient anchored to the pointer position, updated on a single `requestAnimationFrame` per move so it never thrashes layout. Desktop and fine-pointer only, and disabled entirely for reduced-motion.

## What I learned

- Throttle to one `rAF` per `mousemove` — without it the effect can drop frames on big screens.
- Keep the opacity *very* low (~6%). It should be felt more than seen.
- It has to know when to disappear: no glow on touch devices, none when motion is reduced.

This one isn't in a demo box — it's live across the whole site. Move your mouse around and notice the warmth following you.
