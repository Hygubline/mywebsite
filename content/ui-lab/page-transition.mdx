Right now navigating between pages here is instant. I'd like the leaving page to settle down and fade while the new one rises in — a quiet beat that makes the whole site feel like one continuous space.

## Open questions

- Keeping it fast: the transition should shave milliseconds off the *feeling*, never add real delay.
